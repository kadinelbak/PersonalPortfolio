## ForgeLabs Fitness

Mobile health product published on the App Store and Google Play to help users move from biometric and lifestyle data into nutrition, fitness, grocery planning, and daily execution.

## Product Goal

Most nutrition and fitness tools stop at tracking. ForgeLabs Fitness was built around the next step: converting goals, biometric context, meals, exercise, and planning data into actions a user can actually complete.

## Core User Flow

1. Set health, nutrition, or fitness goals.
2. Log meals, exercise, and progress signals.
3. Receive nutrition and meal-planning guidance.
4. Generate grocery lists from the plan.
5. Execute through shopping and daily tracking.

## Shipped Platforms

- [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/forgelabs-fitness/id6752232504)
- [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.forgelabs.fitness)

## Features Highlighted in Store Listings

- Meal logging with barcode scanning, photo recognition, and food database support
- Calories, macros, and micronutrient visibility
- AI-driven recipe and meal planning
- Grocery list generation and shopping support
- Instacart integration noted on Google Play
- Exercise tracking, progress charts, gamification, and coaching support

## Impact

The app reached approximately **100 users** after launch. Its strongest portfolio value is that it moved beyond a prototype: it shipped through both major mobile app stores and connected product thinking, user health data, nutrition guidance, and execution workflows.

## Technical/Product Emphasis

This project demonstrates end-to-end product delivery: defining a health workflow, building mobile features around behavior change, publishing to iOS and Android, and aligning nutrition guidance with grocery execution.

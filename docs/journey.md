    <p>The camera roll starts with new friendships and shared plates, then widens into circuits, 3D printing, travel, research spaces, and family tables. The growth is not a clean straight line. It is more honest than that: learn a little, cook a little better, make something physical, take the photo, say yes to the trip, come back to the people who make the work worth doing.</p>
